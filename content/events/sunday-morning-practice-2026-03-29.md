Our Sunday morning practice offers a longer period of sitting meditation, with space for deeper practice and contemplation.

## Schedule

- **10:00** — Sitting meditation
- **10:30** — Walking meditation
- **10:45** — Sitting meditation

- **12:00** — Close

## A Deeper Practice

While our Wednesday sessions are designed as an introduction, Sunday mornings offer more extended practice time. Newcomers are still welcome — just arrive a few minutes early for a brief orientation.
