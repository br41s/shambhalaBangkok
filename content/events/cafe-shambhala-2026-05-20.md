Join us every Wednesday evening for Café Shambhala — a welcoming space to meditate, learn, and connect. Each session starts with 30 minutes of sitting meditation, followed by an hour of reading from contemplative texts and open discussion.

## Schedule

- **18:30** — Sitting meditation (30 minutes)
- **19:00** — Reading and discussion (1 hour)
- **20:00** — Close

## Who Is It For?

Everyone is welcome, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. No registration required — just show up a few minutes early.

## What to Bring

Just yourself. Cushions and chairs are provided. Wear comfortable clothing.

## Donation

This event is free. A suggested donation helps us maintain the space and continue offering programs.
