Join us every Wednesday evening for an open meditation session. Each session begins with a brief introduction for newcomers, followed by guided sitting meditation and a short period of walking meditation.

## What to Expect

- **19:00** — Welcome and introduction for newcomers
- **19:15** — Sitting meditation (guided)
- **19:45** — Walking meditation
- **20:00** — Sitting meditation
- **20:20** — Discussion and Q&A
- **20:30** — Close

## Who Is It For?

Everyone is welcome, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. No registration required — just show up a few minutes early.

## What to Bring

Just yourself. Cushions and chairs are provided. Wear comfortable clothing.
