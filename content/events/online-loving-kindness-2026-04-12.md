Join us online for a special session on loving-kindness (metta) meditation. This practice cultivates warmth and compassion toward ourselves and others.

- Introduction to loving-kindness practice
- Guided metta meditation
- Sharing and reflection

## How to Join

Click the Zoom link at the scheduled time. We recommend finding a quiet, comfortable space and having your camera on if possible, though it is not required.
