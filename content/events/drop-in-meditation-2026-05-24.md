Join us every Sunday afternoon for Drop In Meditation — one hour of sitting and walking meditation in a calm, supportive environment.

## Schedule

- **13:00** — Sitting meditation
- **13:25** — Walking meditation
- **13:35** — Sitting meditation
- **14:00** — Close

## Who Is It For?

Everyone is welcome, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. No registration required — just show up a few minutes early.

## What to Bring

Just yourself. Cushions and chairs are provided. Wear comfortable clothing.

## Donation

This event is free. A suggested donation helps us maintain the space and continue offering programs.
