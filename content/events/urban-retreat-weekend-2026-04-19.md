A unique two-day urban retreat that combines intensive meditation practice with exercises for bringing mindfulness into the rhythm of city life.

## Who Is It For?

This retreat is suitable for anyone with some meditation experience (at least a few sessions). It is particularly relevant for busy professionals looking to integrate practice into daily urban life.

## What Is Included

- Two full days of guided practice
- Meditation instruction and individual feedback
- Contemplative exercises for urban environments
- Light refreshments and tea
- A take-home practice guide

## Facilitator

Michael Torres is a senior meditation teacher in the Shambhala tradition with 15 years of teaching experience across Southeast Asia.
