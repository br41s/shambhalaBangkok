This half-day workshop is designed for people who have never meditated before or are in the early stages of exploring mindfulness.

## What You Will Learn

- What meditation is (and what it isn't)
- Basic sitting posture and technique
- Working with thoughts and distractions
- How to build a daily practice
- Simple mindfulness exercises for everyday life

## Schedule

- **09:00** — Welcome and introductions
- **09:30** — What is meditation?
- **10:00** — First meditation session (guided)
- **10:30** — Break
- **10:45** — Working with the mind
- **11:30** — Practice session
- **12:00** — Mindfulness in daily life
- **12:30** — Q&A and closing
- **13:00** — End

## About the Facilitator

Sarah Chen has been practicing meditation in the Shambhala tradition for over 10 years and is a certified meditation instructor.
