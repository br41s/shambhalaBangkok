Starting a meditation practice does not require a lot of time, special equipment, or experience. Here are five simple tips to help you begin.

## 1. Start Small

Five minutes a day is enough to begin. Consistency matters more than duration. You can gradually increase your practice time as it becomes more natural.

## 2. Choose a Regular Time

Meditating at the same time each day helps build a habit. Many people find that morning is ideal, but the best time is whatever works for you.

## 3. Find a Quiet Spot

You do not need a special meditation room. A corner of your bedroom, a chair by the window, or even a park bench can work. The key is a place where you feel relatively undisturbed.

## 4. Be Gentle with Yourself

Your mind will wander — that is normal and expected. The practice is not about stopping thoughts, but about noticing when your mind has wandered and gently returning to the breath.

## 5. Connect with a Community

Practicing with others can provide support, motivation, and a sense of shared purpose. Join one of our weekly sessions or connect with our online community.
