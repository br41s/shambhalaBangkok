We are thrilled to present our new website! This redesign focuses on making it easier for you to:

- **Find upcoming events** — our calendar is front and center
- **Connect with the community** — join our WhatsApp or LINE group with one tap
- **Start meditating** — clear guides for first-time visitors
- **Support the centre** — simple donation options

## What's New

The site is now mobile-first, fast, and designed with clarity in mind. You can subscribe to our calendar, get event reminders, and access all the information you need to plan your visit.

## Stay Connected

The best way to stay updated is to join our WhatsApp or LINE community group. You will receive event updates, practice tips, and invitations to special programs.

We look forward to seeing you at the centre soon!
